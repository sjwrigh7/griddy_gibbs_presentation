Discrepancy Term

The discrepancy term is traditionally modeled as a Gaussian process $$\delta(\mathbf{x}) \sim \textrm{GP}(\mu(\mathbf{x}),\Sigma(\mathbf{x},\mathbf{x}'))$$

Likelihood

This gives the joint conditional distribution of the data (the likelihood) $$\begin{align*} \pi(\mathbf{Y}|\boldsymbol{\theta},\boldsymbol{\delta},\sigma^2,\tau^2,\boldsymbol{\rho},\mathbf{X}) \propto \prod_{j=1}^m \bigg[ \left( 2\pi \sigma^2\right)^{-n/2}\\ \exp \left\{- \frac{1}{2\sigma^2} \big[\mathbf{y}_j-\mathbf{g}-\boldsymbol{\delta}\big]^T\big[\mathbf{y}_j-\mathbf{g}-\boldsymbol{\delta}\big]\right\} \bigg] \end{align*} $$
