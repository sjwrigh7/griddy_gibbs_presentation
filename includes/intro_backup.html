Introduction

Piston Fatigue

Fatigue failure is the primary concern for failure of internal combustion engine (ICE) pistons due to cyclic thermal and mechanical loads

  • Combustion pressure
  • Heat softening
  • Thermal stresses

Piston Modeling

Computer models can be used

  • Computational fluid dynamics (CFD) for combustion heat transfer and pressure
  • Finite element analysis (FEA) of piston temperature and stress

Co-simulation

CFD Submodels

Models controlling specific physics details in the main CFD model
  • E.g. spray break-up, evaporation, turbulent dispersion, collision, drop drag, spray-wall interaction, wall splash, turbulence, heat transfer
Details of CFD modeling and submodels described in the work of Ravikumar [27,28]

Co-simulation

CFD Model

Solves the thermodynamics, heat transfer, and fluid dynamics of the combustion event
  • Inputs: submodels, combustion chamber geometry, fuel properties
  • Outputs: pressure, velocity, temperature, heat flux fields in the combustion chamber

Co-simulation

Experimental Testing

  • Wireless telemetry system for piston thermocouples and heat flux probes
  • Additional pressure and heat flux instrumentation in cylinder

Co-simulation

CFD Validation

Validation of CFD model to experimental data by in-cylinder pressure and heat release rate

Co-simulation

CFD Results Mapping

CFD heat transfer results are mapped to the FEA model as a convection boundary condition

Co-simulation

Finite Element Model

  • First order, diffusive, solid tetrahedron elements
  • Steady state heat transfer solution
  • Inputs: CFD mapping, backside boundary conditions, solid material properties
  • Outputs: steady state temperature solution
