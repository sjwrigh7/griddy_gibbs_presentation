Literature Gaps

Bayesian calibration of piston models

  1. No previous publications on calibration of piston model heat transfer boundary conditions quantify the uncertainty of their estimates
  2. There is an inherent lack of computational efficiency for Bayesian model calibration
  3. Identifiability issues due to the discrepancy term

Literature Gaps

Sensor optimization for model calibration

  1. The computational cost of Fisher information-based optimization becomes impractical for computationally expensive computer models
  2. There is little research in efficient domain exploration and exploration of complicated domains (like the piston geometry) for optimal design approaches

Literature Gaps

Piston fatigue analysis

  1. None of the reviewed literature considers flexible estimation of the $S$-$N$ curve and the cumulative damage model
  2. Previous optimal experiment design literature focuses on parametric models and/or constant load amplitude tests
  3. Current accelerated fatigue testing approaches consider only monotonically increasing stresses
